B. Sudarshan — Digital Sports Biography

Overview
A digital portfolio and sports biography dedicated to honoring the career of B. Sudarshan, a legendary Indian Ball Badminton player, 2006 Ekalavya Awardee, and multiple-time National Champion. This project serves as a tribute website that archives his professional journey, achievements, and press coverage spanning over two decades of competitive play.

Live Demo:
https://5059preetham-oss.github.io/portfolio_sudarshan/

Features:-

Detailed Profile: A biographical journey detailing his early life, rise to elite athletics, and career balancing sports with his role as Assistant Manager at Canara Bank.

Career Milestones Timeline: Highlights of major accolades, including the 1992 Junior Gold Medal, Star of India awards (1996 & 2010), the 2006 Ekalavya Award, and the 2012 Senior National Championship.

Interactive Career Analytics: Visual breakdown of his 40+ national tournament appearances, 7-year captaincy tenure, and core attributes (Leadership, Fitness, Agility, and Mentorship).

Media & Archives: A digital gallery featuring tournament memories, press coverage, and Ekalavya Award displays.

Paper Clippings Archive: An interactive gallery housing 96 scanned newspaper clippings compiled from Karnataka State Press and personal collections between 1992 and 2012.
